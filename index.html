01 / THE FESTIVAL

When the
city becomes art.

Durga Puja is not just a festival in Kolkata. It is the season when neighbourhoods become galleries, streets become stages, and every para has a story to tell.

From the clay lanes of Kumartuli to the lights of the pandals, this is a celebration built by artists, artisans, families and millions of visitors.
